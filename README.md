# Autonomous-Vehicle-Cruise-Control-Using-LQG-Optimal-Control-in-MATLAB
The project implements an LQG (Linear Quadratic Gaussian) control system in MATLAB for autonomous vehicle cruise control, maintaining a target speed of 20 m/s under noise and disturbances. The system integrates an LQR controller with a Kalman filter for real-time state estimation. Vehicle dynamics were modeled with a mass of 1000 kg, drag coefficient 0.5, and rolling resistance 0.01, then discretized with a 0.1s timestep. Simulations covered 50 seconds under three scenarios: ideal, noisy measurements, and time-varying disturbances. The LQG controller reduced speed error significantly compared to LQR alone, showing improved robustness and accuracy. This approach demonstrates LQG’s potential for real-world autonomous vehicle control, with plans to extend to multivariable models and varying road conditions.
